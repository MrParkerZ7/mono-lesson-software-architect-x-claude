## 2.1 Role Overview

The **Enterprise Architect** operates at the highest level of technical strategy, defining the overall technology landscape for an organization. They establish standards, principles, and roadmaps that guide all technical decisions across the enterprise. This role bridges the gap between business strategy and technology execution at an organizational scale.

### Detailed Responsibilities

**1. Technology Strategy Development**
- Align technology investments with business objectives
- Define 3-5 year technology vision
- Identify technology capabilities needed for business growth
- Assess build vs. buy vs. partner decisions at enterprise level
- Guide digital transformation initiatives

**2. Standards & Governance**
- Create and maintain architecture principles
- Define technology standards and guidelines
- Establish architecture review processes
- Ensure compliance with regulations (GDPR, SOX, HIPAA)
- Manage technical debt at enterprise level

**3. Enterprise Roadmap**
- Plan technology modernization initiatives
- Coordinate cross-program dependencies
- Balance innovation with operational stability
- Manage application portfolio rationalization

### Sample Enterprise Architecture Principle

```markdown
## Principle: API-First Design

**Statement**:
All new systems and significant modifications must expose capabilities through
well-designed APIs before building user interfaces.

**Rationale**:
- Enables integration across the enterprise
- Supports multi-channel experiences (web, mobile, partner)
- Facilitates reuse and reduces duplication
- Aligns with microservices strategy

**Implications**:
- API design review required before development begins
- API documentation mandatory for all services
- API gateway must be used for external exposure
- API versioning strategy must be followed

**Governance**:
- Architecture Review Board approval required
- API design standards compliance check
- Security review for all external APIs
```

## 2.9 Sample Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Digital Transformation Initiative

**Situation**: A traditional manufacturing company wants to transform into a digital-first organization with IoT, predictive maintenance, and e-commerce capabilities.

**Enterprise Architect Actions**:
1. Assess current technology landscape and capabilities
2. Define target state architecture across all four domains
3. Create 3-year transformation roadmap with phases
4. Identify capability gaps and technology investments
5. Establish cloud-first strategy with hybrid considerations
6. Define data architecture for IoT and analytics
7. Create governance model for transformation
8. Present business case to board with ROI projections

**Deliverables**:
- Current state assessment
- Target state architecture vision
- Business capability roadmap
- Technology investment plan
- Governance framework
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategy

### Scenario 2: Post-Merger Integration

**Situation**: Two mid-size financial services companies are merging and need to rationalize their technology portfolios.

**Enterprise Architect Actions**:
1. Inventory both organizations' application portfolios
2. Identify redundant systems and consolidation opportunities
3. Define target operating model and architecture
4. Create integration roadmap minimizing business disruption
5. Establish unified technology standards
6. Plan data migration and master data management
7. Define organizational structure for combined IT

## 2.10 Comparison with Other Roles

| Aspect | Enterprise Architect | Solution Architect | Chief Architect |
|--------|---------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Scope** | Organization-wide | Project/Product | Strategic vision |
| **Time Horizon** | 3-5 years | 6-18 months | 5-10 years |
| **Hands-On Work** | Minimal | Medium | None |
| **Business Focus** | Very High | High | Executive |
| **Primary Output** | Standards & roadmaps | Solution designs | Strategy & direction |
| **Reports To** | CTO/CIO | EA or Director | CTO/CEO |
| **Team Size** | May manage architects | Individual contributor | Manages EA practice |

## Key Takeaways

- Enterprise Architects **translate business strategy into technology strategy**
- The role requires **exceptional communication skills** to work with executives
- **Governance is a core function** - establishing and enforcing standards
- Success is measured by **organizational outcomes**, not individual projects
- Must balance **innovation with stability** and risk management
- **TOGAF and frameworks** provide structure but must be adapted to context
- The role is **highly strategic** with minimal hands-on technical work

## Practical Exercises

1. **Capability Mapping**: Create a business capability map for a retail organization and identify technology support for each capability.

2. **Architecture Principle Development**: Write 5 architecture principles for a healthcare organization, including rationale and implications.

3. **Portfolio Rationalization**: Given a list of 20 applications, develop criteria for rationalization and recommend retire/invest/maintain decisions.

4. **Roadmap Creation**: Create a 3-year technology roadmap for migrating from on-premises to cloud infrastructure.
